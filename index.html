ABOUT US

Shri Rishabh Vidyoday
Mahavidyalaya

Quality Education   |   Character Building   |   Bright Future

Shri Rishabh Vidyoday Mahavidyalaya is a private affiliated college located in Banahil (Post Nariyara), Akaltara, Janjgir-Champa district, Chhattisgarh.

The college provides a supportive academic environment with learning resources, practical facilities, technology infrastructure and spaces for sports and campus activities.

📅
Established 2019
🏛️
Affiliation Shaheed Nandkumar Patel Vishwavidyalaya, Raigarh
📍
Campus Size 5 Acres
